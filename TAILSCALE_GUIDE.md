# Tailscale Mesh Networking Guide for PiMobile

This guide explains how to configure **Tailscale** to securely connect your mobile phone to your Raspberry Pi devices from anywhere in the world without opening ports or configuring dynamic DNS.

---

## Why Tailscale for Raspberry Pi?

1. **Zero Open Ports**: Normal port forwarding (exposing port 22 or VNC 5900 to the public internet) attracts thousands of automated brute-force attacks daily. Tailscale does not require opening any router ports.
2. **Direct Peer-to-Peer (WireGuard)**: Tailscale utilizes NAT traversal (STUN/ICE/DERP) to negotiate direct, hardware-accelerated WireGuard peer-to-peer tunnels between your phone and your Pi.
3. **Static Mesh IPs (100.x.y.z)**: Even when your Raspberry Pi or phone changes Wi-Fi networks or switches to cellular data (4G/5G), the Tailscale IP and MagicDNS name remain identical.
4. **End-to-End Encryption**: Only your authenticated devices hold the private keys.

---

## Step 1: Install Tailscale on the Raspberry Pi

Log into your Raspberry Pi terminal (either locally or via SSH) and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://tailscale.com/install.sh | sh
```

Authenticate and join your private tailnet:

```bash
sudo tailscale up
```

Follow the printed URL to log in with your identity provider (Google, GitHub, Microsoft, Apple, etc.).

Once logged in, verify your Pi's Tailscale IPv4 address:

```bash
tailscale ip -4
```
*(Example output: `100.94.32.115`)*

---

## Step 2: Install Tailscale on Your Mobile Phone

1. Install **Tailscale** from:
   - **iOS**: [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/tailscale/id1470499037)
   - **Android**: [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tailscale.ipn)
2. Open the app and log in using the **exact same account** used for your Raspberry Pi.
3. Toggle the VPN switch to **Active**.
4. You will see your Raspberry Pi listed in the Tailscale app.

---

## Step 3: Add the Tailscale Endpoint to PiMobile App

1. Open the **PiMobile** mobile app.
2. Tap the **+** (Add) button or edit an existing device.
3. Fill in:
   - **Local LAN Host**: `192.168.1.xxx` or `raspberrypi.local` (used when connected to home Wi-Fi).
   - **Tailscale Mesh IP**: `100.x.y.z` (or your MagicDNS hostname, e.g. `my-pi.tailnet-name.ts.net`).
   - **Agent Auth Token**: The token generated by `./install.sh`.
   - **SSH Username / Password**: Your standard Pi login credentials.
4. Tap **Test Connection**.

The PiMobile app will automatically use the sub-millisecond local network path when you are at home, and seamlessly switch to the encrypted Tailscale path when you are outside on cellular or remote Wi-Fi!
